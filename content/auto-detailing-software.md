Mobile detailing lives or dies on two things: the booking that comes in from Instagram at 9pm, and getting paid before you pull away. Everything below is aimed at those two moments.

## The rest of the job

**Quote the packages, don't retype them.** Interior-only, full correction, ceramic coating — send a quote with your tiers, they accept online, and it converts straight into a scheduled job and then an invoice.

**Time-aware scheduling.** A three-hour correction and a 45-minute maintenance wash aren't the same appointment. Set the start time and duration; the day view lays out your actual working day and warns you before you double-book.

**Every vehicle, remembered.** Notes live with the customer — the black truck with the swirl marks, the client who wants the trim dressed, gate codes for the gated community. Plus every job you've done for them and what you charged.

**Invoice before you leave the driveway.** Mark the job complete, generate the invoice, and it carries a pay link to your own Stripe, PayPal, Square, or Venmo. Money lands in your account; we take no cut of it.

**Maintenance plans, if you sell them.** Monthly or biweekly recurring details schedule themselves, and completed visits can roll up into one monthly invoice per client automatically. Sold a package up front? Record it as account credit and draw it down as you work.

**A real website, included.** Turn on your landing page and you've got a site — before/after gallery, service tiers with prices, reviews, and the booking form — at your own link. For a lot of detailers that replaces paying someone for a site that mostly shows an Instagram feed.

**Your words.** Call them clients, call the work details or appointments — labels change throughout the app.
