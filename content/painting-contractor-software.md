## The rest of the job

**Deposits, handled.** Record the deposit as credit on the customer's account when it comes in. When you send the final invoice, apply the credit and the balance due is what is left.

**A calendar that fits multi-day work.** Set a start time and a duration for each day on site. Day, week and month views, with a warning before you double-book the crew.

**Pay links on every invoice.** Add your Stripe, PayPal, Square or Venmo details and every invoice carries a pay-now link. The money goes straight to your account. We never touch it or take a cut.

**Requests from your website or yard sign.** A booking-request page at your own link, with a QR code for signs and door hangers. Requests land in the app for you to accept or decline.

**Your crew included.** Add your painters at no extra cost. They see the schedule and mark work complete. There is no charge per person and no cap on the number of jobs.
