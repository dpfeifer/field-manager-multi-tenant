If you run a service business by yourself — mowing, cleaning, cutting hair, detailing, handyman work — you've probably looked at Jobber. It's the biggest name in field-service software, and it's a genuinely good product. This page is about whether it's a good product *for you*, or whether you're about to pay for a team-sized tool to do a one-person job.

Full disclosure: we make Field Manager, so we have an obvious stake in your answer. We've tried to earn your trust by being specific about where Jobber is the better choice, because for a lot of businesses it genuinely is.

## The short answer

**Jobber is built for crews.** Dispatching, GPS tracking, per-seat pricing, tiered plans — the product and the pricing both assume you'll add people. **Field Manager is built for the operator who *is* the business.** One flat price, every feature included, nothing that only makes sense with five trucks.

## The pricing difference is structural, not just a number

Jobber charges per user, in tiers — a base plan for one person, then meaningfully more as you add seats or want features that sit in higher plans. Their pricing changes and has promotions, so check their site for current numbers; the structure is the point: **the price scales with heads, and features unlock by tier.**

Field Manager is **{{price}}/month flat**. Every feature, unlimited customers and jobs, and teammates included if you ever add any. It's **free for your first five customers** — no credit card — so you can run real work through it before paying anything.

For a solo operator, the practical difference isn't the first month's bill. It's that with per-seat, per-tier pricing you're always one growth step away from a pricing conversation. Flat means the software bill is a settled question.

## When Jobber is the better fit

Be honest with yourself about these, because getting it wrong costs more than the subscription:

- **You have a crew today, or will within a year.** Dispatching, routing, and GPS are real needs at three-plus field workers, and Jobber does them well.
- **You live in QuickBooks.** Jobber's accounting sync is deep; ours doesn't exist yet.
- **You want built-in card processing** rather than pay-links to your own Stripe/PayPal/Square account.

> If two of those describe you, use Jobber. It's the right tool for that situation, and the per-seat price is buying you something real.

## When Field Manager is the better fit

- **You're solo (or you and one other person) and plan to stay lean.** You'll use maybe a third of what a team platform ships — but you'd pay for all of it.
- **You want the price settled.** {{price}} flat, listed publicly, no sales call, cancel anytime and export your data.
- **You don't have a website.** Every Field Manager account can turn on a hosted landing page — your services, photos, reviews, and booking form at your own link. For a lot of solo operators, that replaces paying for a website separately.
- **You want out of the spreadsheet without a project.** Send us your customer list and we'll import it — customers and upcoming appointments — usually same-day.
