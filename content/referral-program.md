## How the reward works

**A percentage of each completed job.** You choose the percentage. When you mark a job complete for a referred customer, that share of the job's price lands on the referrer's account as credit. It is earned on completion, not on payment, so you are never waiting on an invoice to say thank you.

**Up to a limit you set.** The default is the first five jobs for each referred customer. Raise it, lower it, or remove it. The limit is per referred customer, so a customer who sends you three neighbors earns on all three.

**Credit, not cash.** The reward is credit toward the referrer's own service. It comes off their next invoice automatically, and the invoice shows the line. Nothing leaves your bank account.

**Undo is handled.** If you undo a completion, the credit it earned is taken back.

**Mentioned where it matters.** An optional line at the bottom of every invoice and invoice email carries the customer's own referral link. It never appears on the printed or PDF copy.

A worked example, for a lawn care account rewarding 10% on the first five visits:

| At 10%, first five visits | Amount |
|---|---|
| Weekly mow for the neighbor Heather sent | $65 |
| Heather earns per visit | $6.50 |
| After five visits | $32.50 off Heather's own bill |
| Three neighbors sent | up to $97.50 |

You decide whether 10% is right for your margins. Many operators land between 5% and 15%.

## Setting it up

1. In Settings, open Sections and turn on Referrals. The Referrals screen and the Referrals tab on each customer appear.
2. In Settings, open Referrals and set the percentage and the limit. Send yourself a sample of the thank-you email from the same screen.
3. Open any customer, tap Referrals, and copy their link or email it to them. Each link is made the first time you ask for it.
4. Turn on the invoice line, so every invoice carries the customer's link without you doing anything further.

Takes about two minutes. The Referrals screen in the main menu lists everyone who has referred someone and what they have earned.

## Getting customers to use it

**Ask when they compliment you.** "Glad you like it. If you know anyone who needs the same, here is a link, and you get 10% of their jobs off your bill." Text the link from their Referrals tab while you are standing there.

**Let the invoice do it.** With the invoice line on, every customer sees their link once a month without you saying a word.

**Lean on the thank-you email.** The first time a referrer gets one, they learn the program is real. That is usually when the second referral comes.

**Say it on your booking page and landing page.** A sentence in your description is enough: "Ask us about referral credit."

Q: Does the referral program cost extra?
A: No. It is part of Field Manager on both the Free plan and Pro. There is no add-on and no per-referral fee.
Q: What counts as a referral?
A: A new customer who booked through an existing customer's referral link, or one you marked as referred by picking the referrer from a list when you added them. Field Manager never guesses a referrer from a typed name.
Q: When is the credit earned?
A: When you mark a job complete for the referred customer. The referrer's share of that job's price is added to their account as credit at that moment, and they receive a thank-you email with the new balance.
Q: Can the referrer take the credit as cash?
A: No. The reward is credit toward the referrer's own service with you. It is applied to their next invoice.
Q: Is there a limit on how much one customer can earn?
A: You set it. By default the reward runs for the first five completed jobs of each referred customer. You can raise, lower or remove the limit, and it applies per referred customer, so sending more people earns more.
Q: Can a customer see their own referral balance?
A: Yes. Each referrer has a private page showing their credit, their link and the people who booked through it. The link to it is in every thank-you email, and you can send it from their Referrals tab. If the link ever ends up with the wrong person, reset it and the old one stops working.
Q: What happens if I undo a completed job?
A: The credit that job earned is taken back. If the referrer has already spent it, their balance can go negative until the next job.
Q: Does it work with the booking form on my own website?
A: Yes. Referral links open your Field Manager booking page, and the embedded form on your site carries a referral code when the page it sits on was reached through one.
Q: Can I turn it off?
A: Yes. Turn off the Referrals section in Settings and the screen, tabs and links disappear. Credit already earned stays on customer accounts.
