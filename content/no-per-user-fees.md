Almost every field service platform prices by **seat** — you pay per user, per month. It's a sensible model for software companies and a frustrating one for a two-person crew, because the moment you hire a helper your software bill goes up for work the software isn't doing any more of.

This guide is about how that math actually plays out, and what to look for if you'd rather not sign up for it.

## Why per-seat pricing exists

It's not a scam — it's a proxy. Software vendors need pricing that grows with the value you get, and "number of people using it" is the easiest signal to measure. For a 20-person HVAC company it's roughly fair: more techs really does mean more dispatching, more scheduling, more support burden.

The trouble is that the same model gets applied to the guy with one truck, where it stops tracking value and starts taxing headcount.

## The three costs people miss

**1. The helper tax.** Adding a seasonal helper for the busy months often means paying a full extra seat, every month, for someone who logs in to check their schedule.

**2. The tier cliff.** Per-seat pricing usually rides on top of feature tiers. The feature you actually need — recurring billing, say, or a second user at all — frequently lives one tier up, so the true jump isn't one seat, it's a plan change.

**3. The sales-call tax.** If a pricing page says "contact us," the number is going to depend on what they think you can pay. Public pricing is itself a feature.

## What flat pricing looks like

A flat plan charges one price for the account, not the person. In practice, look for these specifics — the phrase "flat rate" alone doesn't mean much:

- **Are teammates included, or just cheaper?** "Additional users $X" is still per-seat.
- **Is every feature in the one price**, or is there a Pro tier holding the useful half?
- **Is the number public?** If you have to ask, it isn't fixed.
- **Can you export and leave?** Flat pricing means nothing if your customer list is hostage.
- **Is there a real free tier**, so you can run actual work through it before paying?
