Field Manager is built and run by one person. I have run a lawn care business in the mid-Atlantic for three years, and this is the software I run it on.

## Why it exists

When I started, I tried the well-known field-service tools. They were built for companies with a dispatcher, an office and a sales team. I had a truck, a route and a phone. I was paying for screens I never opened, and being asked to pay more for every person I added.

So I built what I needed for my own use: a customer list with notes, a calendar that repeats, a button to mark a job done, and invoices that build themselves at the end of the month.

I have used it every week since. I like it, and I think it is useful to other people who run a small service business the way I do. That is the whole reason it is for sale.

## What that means for you

**It is small on purpose.** No dispatch board, no route optimisation, no inventory. If you need those, a bigger platform is the right choice, and the [comparison pages](/learn) say so plainly.

**The price is flat.** One monthly price with your whole crew included. I did not like being charged per user, so I do not do it.

**A person answers.** Help and support inside the app goes to me, and so does a reply to any email you get from Field Manager. There is no ticket queue.

**Your data is yours.** Export everything as CSV at any time. If you leave, you take it with you.

**I will set it up for you.** Send your customer list in any form, a spreadsheet, an export from another app, or photos of a notebook, and I will load it, free, usually the same day.

## Why there is no name or photo here

I run a small local business and prefer to keep it separate from this one for now. What I can offer instead is a product you can try without paying, a [live demo](/demo) that needs no sign-up, and a reply from the person who built it whenever you write.
