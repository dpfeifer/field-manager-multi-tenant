Housecall Pro is a serious platform for home service companies — dispatching, marketing automation, payment processing, consumer financing, the works. The question this page answers is narrower: **if you're one person with a truck, are you buying capability or overhead?**

We make Field Manager, so we have an obvious bias here. We've tried to counter it by being specific about what Housecall Pro does that we simply don't — there's a whole section on it below.

## The shape of the difference

Housecall Pro is built around **growing into a team**. Plans tier by number of users, and the features that justify the upper tiers — dispatching, advanced reporting, marketing automation — only start earning their keep once you have people to dispatch and enough volume to market to.

Field Manager is **{{price}} a month, flat, with everything included**. There's no upper tier to graduate into, because nothing is held back for one.

So for a one-person business the real question isn't which first invoice is smaller. It's whether you'll ever use the half of the platform you're paying for.

## When Housecall Pro is the right call

- **You have a crew, or you're about to.** Dispatching, live technician tracking, and assigning work across several people are things Housecall Pro does well. We barely do them at all.
- **You want payments and accounting under one roof.** Housecall Pro processes cards, offers customer financing, and syncs deeply with QuickBooks. We do none of that — we hand payment off to whatever processor you already use.
- **You want marketing that runs itself.** Postcards, email campaigns, review requests, automated appointment reminders. We don't have any of it.
- **You need to plug into other software.** They have a large integrations marketplace. We don't have one.

> If two or more of those sound like you, the higher price is buying something real, and you should pay it.

## When Field Manager is the right call

- **You work alone, or with one helper.** Everything in the list above is built for coordinating a crew you don't have yet.
- **You want the price settled.** {{price}} flat, published on the website, no sales call and no quote form. Cancel and export your data whenever you like.
- **You don't have a website.** A hosted page with your services, photos, reviews, and booking form comes with the account. For a lot of one-person operations, that's a second bill they can stop paying.
- **You bill for recurring work.** Set a weekly, biweekly, or monthly schedule once. Mark each visit complete from your phone. At month end, every completed visit rolls into one invoice per customer automatically.
- **You want to keep your own payment processor.** Invoices carry a pay link to your Stripe, PayPal, Square, or Venmo. The money goes straight to you and we take no percentage of it.

## The honest summary

Housecall Pro is a good product built for companies that intend to become bigger companies. If that's your plan, start there — you'll grow into it rather than out of it.

But plenty of people are running lean on purpose, and mostly need the schedule, the customer book, the invoices, and a website that doesn't embarrass them. That's the whole of what we do, at a price that never moves.
