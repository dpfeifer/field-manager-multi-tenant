Housecall Pro is a serious platform for home service companies — dispatching, marketing automation, payment processing, consumer financing, the works. The question this page answers is narrower: **if you're one person with a truck, are you buying capability or overhead?**

We make Field Manager, so read accordingly. There's a real "use Housecall Pro instead" section below.

## The shape of the difference

Housecall Pro is priced and built around **growth into a team**: plans tier by number of users, and the features that justify the higher tiers — dispatching, advanced reporting, marketing automation — only start paying off once you have people to dispatch and volume to market to.

Field Manager is **$29/month flat, forever, everything included**. There is no higher tier to graduate into, because there's nothing held back.

For a solo operator the practical question isn't the first invoice. It's whether you'll use the half of the platform you're paying for.

## When Housecall Pro is the right call

- **You have or are hiring a crew.** Real dispatching, live tech tracking, and job assignment across multiple people are things it does well and we do minimally.
- **You want payments, financing, and payroll in one platform.** Housecall Pro processes cards, offers consumer financing, and integrates deeply with QuickBooks. We do none of that — we hand off to your own processor.
- **You want built-in marketing automation.** Postcards, email campaigns, review requests, automated reminders. We don't have these.
- **You need a large integrations ecosystem.** They have one; we don't.

Two or more of those describing you means the extra cost is buying something real.

## When Field Manager is the right call

- **You're solo, or you and a helper.** The features above have no one to point at yet.
- **You want the bill settled and public.** $29 flat, no sales call, no quote form, cancel and export whenever.
- **You want a website included.** A hosted landing page with your services, photos, reviews, and booking form comes with the account — for a lot of one-person operations that's a separate bill eliminated.
- **You bill recurring work.** Set weekly, biweekly, or monthly schedules; mark visits complete from your phone; roll a month of completed visits into one invoice per customer automatically.
- **You want to keep your own processor.** Pay links to your Stripe, PayPal, Square, or Venmo — we take no percentage.

## The honest summary

Housecall Pro is a good product aimed at companies that intend to become bigger companies. If that's the plan, start there. If you're running lean on purpose and mostly need the schedule, the client book, the invoices, and a website — that's the whole of what we do, at a price that doesn't move.
